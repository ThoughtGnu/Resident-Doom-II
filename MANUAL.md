## Controls

**Moving (fixed cameras)**

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| Up / W | walk forward |
| Down / S | back up |
| Left, Right / A, D | turn |
| Shift (hold) | run |
| CapsLock | always run on / off (then Shift walks) |
| Space | jump |
| C (hold) | crouch |

**Fighting**

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| Ctrl / right mouse | aim (hold) |
| E while aiming | fire |
| Up / Down | aim up / down (while aiming) |
| 1-7, mouse wheel | switch weapons (All Weapons cheat only; 7 = flamethrower) |

Without the cheat, equip a weapon in the inventory.

**Mac:** hold Control to aim and press E to fire. On a trackpad, a two-finger click also aims.

**Field of View:** Settings > Video > Field of View (first person, 75-120).

**Actions**

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| E | pick up, open doors, use typewriters and the item box |
| Tab / I | inventory (pauses the game) |
| M | map screen (pauses the game) |

**View and other keys**

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| V | first person / fixed cameras |
| L | lighting: Doom / RE1 |
| F6 | quicksave |
| Esc | main menu |
| ~ (tilde) | console (for cheat codes) |

**First person (after V)**

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| Mouse | look around |
| Q / R | step left / right |
| Left mouse | fire while aiming (or E) |

Your current keys: **Settings > Controls**. To change them: **Settings >
Customize Controls**.

## Characters

| | Jill Valentine | Chris Redfield |
|---|---|---|
| Health | 96 | 140 |
| Item slots | 8 | 6 |
| Aiming | normal | turns faster while aiming |
| Flamethrower | no | yes (only Chris can use it) |

Both start with a knife, a Beretta (15 rounds) and a First Aid Spray. Nothing
is equipped at first: press Tab, pick the Beretta and choose **EQUIP**.

**AI partner:** on the character select screen, **BOTH** gives you a partner.
Pick who you play; the other one follows you and fights.
- The partner can't be hurt, never runs out of ammo, and you can walk through
  each other. They open normal doors to keep up, key doors while you carry
  the key, and secret doors you've already found, but never switches or
  lifts. They crawl through low gaps on hands and knees, like you.
- To give them a better gun, stand close, open the inventory, pick the gun and
  choose **GIVE TO**. They hand you back the gun they had.
- **Settings > Gameplay > AI partner:** Off means no partner and no BOTH
  choice.
- **Settings > Gameplay > Partner pickups:** On (RE5 style) and, when no enemy
  is in sight, they walk over to ammo for their own gun near your path, pick
  it up and come back. You get it with their gun when you swap.
- **Settings > Gameplay > Starting view:** third or first person at every
  start (new game, level, loaded save, Continue). V still switches any time.
  New Game also asks it after you pick the character; your choice changes
  this setting.
- **Settings > Gameplay > Flat corpses:** dead monsters lie flat on the floor
  instead of turning to face you, so you can walk around them. First person
  only (default), both views, or Off for Doom's usual corpses.

## Items

### Herbs, healing and poison

| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| Green Herb | restores a third of your health |
| Red Herb | does nothing alone; mix it with a green one |
| Blue Herb | cures poison |
| First Aid Spray | restores all of your health |

A healing item is only used up if it does something.

**Mixing:** in the inventory, pick a herb, choose **COMBINE**, then pick the
other herb. Mixing saves slots.

| Mix | What it does |
|---|---|
| Green + Green | two thirds of your health |
| Green + Red | all of your health |
| Green + Blue | a third, and cures poison |
| 3 Greens | all of your health |
| 2 Greens + Blue | two thirds, and cures poison |
| Green + Red + Blue | all, and cures poison |

**Poison:** Arachnotrons and the Spider Mastermind poison you.
- Like RE1, you lose 2 health every 4 seconds (the first time about 5
  seconds after you're poisoned), and the inventory shows **POISON** in
  purple.
- Poison alone stops at 1 health, but any hit after that can kill you.
- A Blue Herb, or a mix with one, cures it.

### Other items

- **Typewriters and Ink Ribbons:** every map has a typewriter near the start,
  and long maps have a second one about halfway. Face it and press E to save.
  Each Ink Ribbon you find is one save in Classic RE; they stack 3 to a slot. There is
  one by the start typewriter on every map and one by each halfway typewriter.
  See [Saving](#saving).
- **Item box:** the wooden chest near the start of every map. Face it and press E;
  the lid opens and the box screen appears. All boxes
  share the same 48 slots, so what you store waits for you on the next map.
- **Keys:** Doom's keycards and skull keys are RE1 keys and crests (Armor,
  Sword, Shield and Helmet Keys, Star and Sun Crests). Walk over them. They
  take no slot and open the doors of their colour.
- **Map:** press M for the map screen. It shows what you've seen; the MAP item
  shows the whole level. Arrows move, + / - or the wheel zoom, M or Esc closes.
- **Chemical:** protects you from toxic floors for 1 minute.
- **Lighter:** a small flame that lights the dark around you for 2 minutes.

The Chemical and the Lighter work as soon as you pick them up. Their time left
shows at the top left and blinks orange near the end.

## Weapons

To use a weapon: press Tab, pick it, choose **EQUIP**. Hold Aim, then press E
to fire. To reload, **COMBINE** its ammo with the gun.

| Weapon | Holds (damage) |
|---|---|
| Knife | never runs out (8) |
| Beretta | 15 rounds (9) |
| Shotgun | 7 shells, hits a wide area (53) |
| Colt Python | 6 magnum rounds (130) |
| Bazooka | 6 explosive (201), acid or flame (95) rounds; COMBINE another kind of round to switch |
| Rocket launcher | 4 rockets, can't be reloaded (900) |
| Flamethrower | Chris only. 240 fuel, 9 seconds of flame. Hold Aim and hold E. Another flamethrower fills it up. |

Damage is RE1's, against a zombie.

**Doom's weapons become:**

| Doom | Resident Doom |
|---|---|
| Pistol | Beretta |
| Shotgun | Shotgun |
| Super shotgun | Colt Python |
| Rocket launcher | Bazooka (explosive) |
| Plasma rifle | Bazooka (acid) |
| BFG9000 | Rocket launcher |
| Chaingun | Flamethrower (Chris), a clip (Jill) |
| Chainsaw | Ammo for a gun you carry |

Picking up a gun you already have gives you its ammo.

## Saving

Choose the save mode in **Settings > Gameplay > Save mode**.

| Save mode | How it works |
|---|---|
| Normal (default) | save any time: Save Game, F6 or a typewriter |
| Typewriter | save only at a typewriter; no ribbon needed |
| Classic RE | save only at a typewriter, and each save uses an Ink Ribbon, like RE1. A new game starts with none: find them by the typewriters. |

In Typewriter and Classic RE, Save Game and F6 only tell you to find a
typewriter.

**If you die**, press **Space or E** to continue, like RE1's Continue:
- **Saved on this level** (any save mode): you go back to your most recent save.
- **Not yet:** the level starts again, with exactly the items, ammo, health and
  armor you had when you entered it.

Continuing never uses an Ink Ribbon. The partner comes back with you.

- **At a typewriter:** face it and press E, then pick a save slot. If you back
  out without saving, you keep your Ink Ribbon.
- **Loading:** main menu > Load Game. GZDoom also saves by itself at the start
  of each map.

## Inventory style

**Settings > Gameplay > Inventory style** changes how the status screen (Tab)
and the item box screen look. Both styles do exactly the same things.

| Style | Look |
|---|---|
| Resident Doom (default) | the mod's own screen |
| RE1 Classic | RE1's own status screen: its item pictures, the character's portrait, the ECG (Fine / Caution / Danger / Poison in RE1's colours), the item slots (Jill 8, Chris 6), and the MAP / FILE / EXIT tabs above the slots. MAP opens the map, FILE this Manual and EXIT closes the screen; RADIO stays blank. The item box screen is RE1's too. |

In RE1 Classic, Up from the top row of slots goes to the tabs.
## Lighting

Press L, or use **Settings > Gameplay > Lighting**. The game remembers your
choice.

| Mode | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| Doom (default) | the levels lit exactly as Doom made them |
| RE1 | darker rooms everywhere, and a faint light that follows you. Items stay just visible. |

The Lighter (see [Items](#other-items)) lights up the dark around you in both
modes. Muzzle flashes look the same in both modes (a small light at the barrel; it never lights up the characters).

## Records

**Main menu > Extras > Records:** your best clear of each map, kept on this PC.

- **What is kept:** for each map, character and difficulty, your fastest time,
  with that run's kills, secrets, items, saves made and the date.
- **When a map counts:** when you reach its exit (MAP30: when you destroy the
  Icon of Sin). The level-end screen says **NEW RECORD**. The Mansion has no
  exit yet, so it has no record.
- **Cheats:** a run where any cheat was on, even for a moment, sets no record.
- **When records are saved:** new records are written to disk when you quit
  normally: with **Quit Game** or by closing the window. Only a crash loses that
  session's new records.
- Records can be cleared on the Records screen (**CLEAR RECORDS**).

## Cheats

**Main menu > Extras > Cheats.** Cheats switch off when you quit, and a run with a cheat
on sets no record.

| In the Cheats menu | What it does |
|---|---|
| Infinite Ammo | guns never run out |
| Invincibility | no damage |
| Super Jump | much higher jumps |
| All Weapons | every weapon, fully loaded, on keys 1-7 and the wheel (7 = flamethrower, Chris only); ammo and fuel run out as usual (only Infinite Ammo is endless) |

**Cheat codes:** open the console (~), type a code and press Enter.

| Code | What it does |
|---|---|
| `starsteam` | invincibility on / off |
| `rocketman` | all weapons |
| `jillsandwich` | full health |
| `inkribbon` | 3 Ink Ribbons |
| `re_jillflamer` | gives Jill the flamethrower (normally Chris only) |
| `re_castcall` | the ending cast call; `re_castcall_jill`, `_chris`, `_both` show one version |

**Cheats > Cheat List** shows every cheat and its console command.
